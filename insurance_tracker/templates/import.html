Import insurer spreadsheets

Upload the Excel reports you get from the insurance company. The app recognises:

  • Renewal listings (LOB, POLICY, RENEWAL DATE, CLIENT NAME…): adds or updates clients and policies, sets each renewal date and the premium due, and creates a reminder for any outstanding premium.
  • Commission statements (ProdCode, InvPrefix, PolicyNo, InvoiceNo, Comm%, CommAmt…): records each commission line and fills in policy terms and commission rates.

You can import the same file more than once. Clients are matched by name, policies by policy number and commissions by invoice number, so nothing is doubled up.
